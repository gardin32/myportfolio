Frequently Asked Questions

General

  • What services do you offer? We design and print banners, signs, and a range of other marketing materials, including vinyl banners, retractable banner stands, yard signs, posters, decals, and business signage.
  • Do I need my own design, or can you create one for me? Either works. You can send us a print-ready file, or our design team can create custom artwork for you based on your brand, message, and any images or logos you provide.
  • What file formats do you accept for print-ready artwork? We prefer high-resolution PDF files with fonts outlined or embedded. We also accept PSD, PNG, and JPG files, though vector formats print sharper, especially for large-format banners.

Pricing & Ordering

  • How much does a banner cost? Pricing depends on size, material, finishing options (grommets, hemming, pole pockets), and quantity. Contact us with your specs and we'll send a quote, or check our online pricing calculator if available.
  • Is there a minimum order quantity? No, we can print a single banner or sign. Discounts are available for bulk and repeat orders.

Design & Proofs

  • Will I see a proof before printing? Yes. We send a digital proof for approval before any job goes to print. Production doesn't begin until you sign off.
  • Can I make changes after approving a proof? Minor changes may be possible before production starts, but once a job enters the print queue, changes may incur additional costs or delays.

Materials & Specs

  • What banner materials are available? Common options include 13oz and 18oz vinyl, mesh (for windy areas), fabric, and pole banner material. We can recommend the right material based on where and how the banner will be used.
  • Indoor or outdoor — does it matter? Yes. Outdoor banners typically need weather-resistant materials and reinforced finishing like grommets or hemming, while indoor signage has more flexibility in materials and finishes.

Turnaround & Delivery

  • How long does production take? Standard turnaround is typically 2–5 business days depending on order size and complexity, not including shipping time.
  • Do you offer local pickup or delivery? Yes, local pickup is available at our shop, and we also offer shipping nationwide.

Support

  • What if there's a problem with my order? Contact us right away. We stand behind our print quality and will work with you to correct any errors on our end.
  • How do I get a quote or place an order? Reach out through our contact form, email, or phone, and include your size, quantity, and design details. We'll follow up with pricing and next steps.
